## Decisions
- **D?download-backoff** · 2026-10-24 · A failed download retries at 1s, 5s, 15s, then settles at every 60s,
  uncapped; an explicit Retry skips the wait but keeps the attempt count. *Why:* matches the Downloads screen's
  own line ("tries again on its own in a minute", `design/layouts/DECISION.md`) without inventing a new schedule.
- **D?wifi-gate-setting-only** · 2026-10-24 · Wi-Fi-only (A6) gates the queue on the `wifiOnly` setting alone;
  `expo-network` isn't a dependency, so whether the device is actually on Wi-Fi isn't checked yet. *Why:* A6 asks
  only that the setting default on and hold downloads back; real detection waits for 05.1-05.4/05.8's real sources.
