## Decisions
- **D?sleep-follow-position** · 2026-10-28 · The End of chapter sleep timer retargets from the observed
  position (`sleepFollowPosition`, `src/lib/timeline.ts`), not only explicit seek calls. *Why:* a native
  lock-screen or media-key seek never calls `followSleep`.
