# Soundscape loops

The bed's ambient soundscapes (01.7), in the mixer's chip order. Every one is synthesized from seeded noise,
filters and simple tones by `scripts/make-soundscapes.mjs` (the recipes are `scripts/soundscapes/synth.mjs`): nothing
is sampled, recorded or downloaded, so no third-party material and no third-party license is involved. Each is
this project's own work, owned by the project, and may be played in the app (agents.md rule 6, spec 01 A18).

The café's murmur is noise shaped by vowel-like formants: no words, no voice, no melody.

**Format.** Mono 16-bit PCM WAV at 16 kHz, 24 s each (750 KB; 4.5 MB for the six). WAV is sample-exact in every
decoder, so the wrap is exactly the seam the synthesis made. AAC (`.m4a`) was tried and not used: ffmpeg's encoder
adds 1024 samples of priming silence (64 ms here) that only an edit-list-aware decoder trims, which would be a
drop-out at every wrap wherever the player ignores the edit list.

**Seamless.** Every slow modulation completes whole cycles in 24 s, every filter runs circularly (a settling pass
over the loop, then the kept pass), and every one-off event (droplet, crackle, syllable, clink) wraps from the end
to the start. `scripts/__tests__/soundscapes.test.mjs` checks each file's wrap (step, kink and level against the
loop's own) and that the files are byte for byte what the script writes. To rebuild:
`node scripts/make-soundscapes.mjs`.

`test-low-hum.wav` and `test-drift.wav` are the bed's placeholder loops (01.3): quiet sine tones made by
`scripts/make-test-loops.mjs`, also this project's own work. The six above replace them.
