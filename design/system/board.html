Mood Read — design system board (spec 06, chunk 06.1)

Three options inside the owner's direction, full gen z chaotic: clashing saturated hues, oversized display type, sticker-like chips, playful motion and voice. Dark only. Every text pair drawn here clears WCAG 2.2 AA; emotion chips always carry their label. Same fake chapter content as spec 03's boards (invented moods, not real scoring). Option A is Recommended and its values are the ones in src/ui/theme.ts.

Recommended

Acid sticker

Near-black violet ground, acid lime against hot pink, eight loud emotion hues. Chips are die-cut stickers: ink outline, off-white hard shadow, a few degrees of tilt. Solid fills only.
